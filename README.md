
# Hospital Operations & Business Intelligence Analytics

## Project Overview

An end-to-end Healthcare Data Analytics project focused on transforming raw hospital data into actionable business insights. Using Excel, Power Query, PostgreSQL, SQL, and Power BI, I analysed patient activity, doctor performance, hospital operations, waiting times, service utilisation, and financial performance.

The project followed the complete analytics process: **Data Cleaning → Transformation → Analysis → Modelling → Visualisation → Insights → Recommendations.**

## Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Excel:** Data cleaning, PivotTables, PivotCharts, and exploratory analysis.
- **Power Query:** Data transformation and preparation.
- **SQL & PostgreSQL:** Database management and business analysis.
- **Power BI:** Data modelling, DAX measures, and interactive dashboards.
- **DAX:** KPI development and analytical calculations.

## Project Workflow

### 1. Business Understanding
- Identified business problems and key stakeholders.
- Developed 15+ business questions.
- Defined analytical objectives across patients, doctors, locations, operations, and financial performance.

### 2. Data Cleaning & Feature Engineering
- Cleaned and transformed four related tables: Visits, Patients, Doctors, and Locations.
- Identified and resolved data quality issues.
- Investigated date inconsistencies and documented corrective actions.
- Created 5+ derived columns to support deeper analysis.
- Maintained a data quality log.

### 3. Excel Analysis
- Developed PivotTables and PivotCharts.
- Analysed patient demographics, doctor workload, location performance, waiting times, department performance, and revenue trends.

### 4. SQL Analysis
- Loaded cleaned datasets into PostgreSQL.
- Answered 15+ business questions using SQL.
- Applied filtering, aggregation, joins, subqueries, and grouping techniques.
- Translated query results into business interpretations.

### 5. Power BI Data Modelling & DAX
- Designed a Star Schema data model.
- Established fact and dimension tables and appropriate relationships.
- Developed 15+ DAX measures covering patient activity, financial performance, and operational efficiency.

### 6. Power BI Dashboard Development

Developed a four-page interactive Power BI report:

- **Executive Overview:** Hospital performance KPIs and key trends.
- **Patient & Service Analysis:** Patient demographics, activity, and service utilisation.
- **Doctor & Location Performance:** Doctor workload and location performance.
- **Operations & Financial Performance:** Waiting times, operational efficiency, and financial performance.

**Dashboard Features:**
- Interactive slicers and filters.
- Drill-through and drill-down functionality.
- Custom tooltips.
- Visual interactions.
- Conditional formatting.
